# DrugCon-Bench

DrugCon-Bench is an exhaustively annotated benchmark of **542 contradictory
drug–disease claim pairs** drawn from **819 RCT abstracts** (PubMed Central Open
Access subset, 2016–2025). Every claim pair about the same drug–disease
association within these 819 abstracts was reviewed, so the benchmark supports
closed-world evaluation of both contradiction detection and contradiction
explanation.

Each contradictory pair is labelled with one or more of ten study-level
contextual factors that may explain the disagreement.

## Construction

1. **Collection.** RCT abstracts published 2016–2025 in the PMC Open Access
   subset, identified with the Cochrane RCT filter (224,756 abstracts).
2. **Claim extraction.** Conclusion sentences identified with a rhetorical
   sentence classifier (Lan et al., 2024).
3. **Claim pairing.** Claims sharing at least one MeSH-normalised drug and
   disease (PubTator 3) were paired, then filtered at BioHiCL-Base cosine ≥ 0.7
   (609,877 candidate pairs over the full corpus).
4. **Detection.** DrugCon with GPT-4.1-nano flagged 615 pairs, spanning 819
   abstracts; annotators confirmed 494.
5. **Exhaustive review.** All remaining candidate pairs within the 819 abstracts
   were reviewed; 48 further contradictions were confirmed by two annotators,
   giving 542 pairs.

Pairs proposed during exhaustive review but not confirmed by the second
annotator (5 pairs) are not in the gold set and count as negatives in
`detection.csv`.

## ManConCorpus

The external generalisation benchmark, ManConCorpus (Alamri & Stevenson, 2016),
is not redistributed here. Obtain it from the original publication. For the
paper we converted its question–claim pairs into abstract–abstract pairs
(contradictory iff the two claims answer the same question differently) and
used a question-level split of 11 / 6 / 8 questions (train / dev / test).
